"assumed" marks a node as a guess the person has not confirmed. They answer it in the gallery: right, or wrong and what is right instead. To answer, they need what was guessed and what else it could be, so say both, in a whole sentence:

```json
"assumed": "Card fields are drawn here; the real build may use the payment provider's hosted fields"
```

check refuses one too short to say that: empty, or a word or two (fewer than 5 words, or than 10 characters in Chinese or Japanese). Write it in the language you speak with the person, whatever the description's "locale" is. Take it out once the person confirms it.

The assumptions underdraw makes itself, a navigate to a screen not drawn yet and a component it does not know, come as whole sentences naming that screen or component, in English, Chinese or Japanese as the command that reports them is asked: --lang en|zh|ja on `check <root>`, `render` and `gallery`, English by default, whatever the machine's locale.

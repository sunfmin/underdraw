WHEN
  After `underdraw check` answers "ok": true, to read the node boxes and
  know where each part went, at a phone width (390) and a desktop width
  (1120). What a person looks at is the gallery (`underdraw gallery`), not
  these PNGs.

WHO
  The agent that writes the description.

READS
  <path>/screen.json, its tokens.json (spaces, radii, fonts, breakpoints;
  the nearest at or above <path>, as `underdraw --help` says) and, beside
  it, kinds.json when there is one (what the project's own
  kinds look like). Refuses a description `check` finds faults in. With
  --root, also the "screen" of every description under that directory, as
  `check <root>` reads them: a node that navigates to a screen none of them
  declares is assumed, a screen not drawn yet. Without it, navigates are
  not resolved.

WRITES
  One PNG: --out, or <dir>/renders/<fixture or phase>-<width>.png. Running
  it again overwrites the same file.

ANSWERS
  {
    "png":        the file written
    "project":    the screen's project root: the nearest directory at or
                  above it holding domain.json, or its own directory
    "tokens":     the tokens.json it took, or null when there is none
    "state":      {"fixture": name} or {"phase": name}
    "breakpoint": "compact" or "regular", chosen by tokens' breakpoints and --width
    "width", "height": the page in points; the page is as tall as its content
    "scale":      pixels per point (lowered when the page is taller than the GPU draws)
    "pixels":     [width, height] of the PNG
    "nodes":      [{"id", "kind", "box": {"x", "y", "width", "height"} in points,
                    "label": the number tagged on it in the picture, or null,
                    "assumed": what the agent guessed there, or that its kind is
                      not one underdraw knows, or (given --root) that it
                      navigates to a screen not drawn yet, or null; what
                      underdraw assumes itself is a whole sentence in
                      --lang, en or zh, en by default}, …]
  }

WHAT YOU SEE
  A wireframe, in grays: boxes, text in the system font, crossed boxes for
  images. A dark numbered circle tags everything a person can act on, in
  document order; the same number is "label" in nodes, so "3" in a
  conversation means one node. An orange dashed outline and "?" mark what
  was assumed, for the person to confirm, and a kind underdraw does not
  know, drawn as a box with its name. What a dialog (an opaque box pinned
  over the page) or its backdrop covers gets no number and no "?": its
  "label" is null, and its "assumed" still says why it was guessed. Icons
  are Material Symbols, drawn outlined, or solid for a name ending in .fill.

WHAT TRIPS YOU UP
  - Needs a GPU (Metal on macOS); `underdraw doctor` says whether there is one.
  - The page shows everything at once: a sticky bar is drawn at the end of
    the page, not over the content.
